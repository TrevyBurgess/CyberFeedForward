Security operations have a direction problem. Most tooling tells you what
already happened: an alert fires, an analyst triages, a ticket moves through
the queue. Useful — but always one step behind.

## Feeding forward

A feedback loop corrects after the fact. A *feed-forward* loop anticipates.
We analyze actor behavior, tooling trends, and infrastructure patterns to
project what your threat landscape looks like next quarter — not what it
looked like last night.

## What you'll find here

- **Briefings** — concise analysis of campaigns and actors relevant to your sector
- **Engineering notes** — practical detection and hardening guidance
- **Research** — deeper dives into emerging tradecraft
